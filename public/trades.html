FAQ

Tradies ask us…

What format works for trades?

The digital file scales anywhere: print business-card size for handovers, A5 for the office, or drop the PNG into your invoice template. The landscape format suits cards best.

Can I put it on the ute?

Yes — buy the digital file and any signwriter can print it as a decal. Keep the QR at least 10 cm square for scanning at a glance, and test-scan the proof before it goes on.

I do jobs across town — where does the sign live?

The engraved sign suits your counter or office if customers visit; otherwise most tradies use cards + the invoice PNG. Same design, three placements.

Does the QR ever expire?

No — it encodes Google's own review link for your business. No subscription, nothing to renew, works as long as your Google listing exists.
