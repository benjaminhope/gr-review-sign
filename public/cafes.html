FAQ

Café owners ask us…

Where should the sign go?

The register is the classic spot — everyone pauses there to pay. Table talkers work well for table service, and a second sign near the door catches takeaway regulars.

Can it match our brand colours?

Yes — every colour on the sign is editable, including the QR itself. Start from a template and adjust, or build from scratch. The built-in scan check makes sure it still scans.

Is asking for reviews allowed?

Asking is fine; incentivising isn't. A neutral "enjoyed your coffee? tell Google" sign is exactly what Google expects businesses to do.

What sizes work best?

A5 suits counters; A6 table talkers for tables. The print pack includes A5, A4 and 5×7″ at 300 DPI, and the engraved sign is ~150×100 mm with a stand.
