# ReviewSign — image-generation prompt kit

Each design below is written as a paste-ready prompt for an AI image generator
(Midjourney / DALL-E / Imagen style). They describe the *physical product*, so
outputs look like listing photos, not vector art.

## How to use

1. Take the **base scene** paragraph, then append one **design block**.
2. Keep the QR wording as-is — generators can't draw a real QR; you want a
   "plausible QR-looking square" which you later replace/overlay with the real
   QR from the designer (export the PNG and composite it in any editor).
3. Generate square (1:1) for Etsy/Instagram, 4:5 for the website cards.

**Base scene (prepend to every prompt):**

> Professional product photograph of a laser-engraved acrylic counter sign,
> standing perfectly vertical in a transparent clear acrylic channel stand so
> it appears to float slightly above the surface, on a warm timber reception
> counter in a softly lit cafe, shallow depth of field, natural window light
> from the left, photorealistic, no people, no brand logos.

**Consistency tags (append to every prompt):**

> product photography, 85mm lens look, soft shadows, neutral warm colour
> grade, e-commerce listing photo

## Notes

- Generators will invent a fake QR pattern — that's fine for mood shots; for
  anything a customer might actually scan, composite the real QR PNG from the
  designer over the generated one.
- If a generator struggles with "engraved", add: "the lettering is recessed
  into the acrylic surface, revealed core colour, slight depth and shadow in
  the engraving".
- The floating stand: if it renders as a solid block, add "the stand is a
  thin transparent acrylic channel, barely visible, the sign appears to
  hover".
